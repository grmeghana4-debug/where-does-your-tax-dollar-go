ABOUT THIS PROJECT

Follow the money. Understand the numbers.

Where Does Your Tax Dollar Go? is an independent educational project designed to make U.S. federal spending data easier to explore. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the U.S. government or any political party.

What the calculator means

The calculator applies the shares of reported federal spending obligations by budget function in the selected USAspending Explorer period to an example amount. It does not trace an individual's tax payment, and federal spending is financed by multiple revenue sources and borrowing.

Editorial approach

We aim to link primary sources, explain the calculation, distinguish obligations from outlays and receipts, and avoid presenting opinion as fact. Figures may change as source agencies update or certify their reports.
