READ THE FINE PRINT

One dollar is a model, not a receipt.

Federal money is collected from many sources—individual and corporate income taxes, payroll taxes, excise taxes, fees, and borrowing. The government does not assign each taxpayer's exact dollar to a specific program.

This calculator takes each budget function's share of the total reported obligations in the selected USAspending Explorer period, then applies those percentages to the amount you enter. It is a way to understand the spending mix, not personal tax advice or a literal tracing of tax payments.

01
Get the public data

Request the budget-function breakdown from the USAspending API.

02
Calculate each share

Divide each function's reported amount by the total reported amount.

03
Scale your example

Multiply each share by the dollar amount you enter.
